# Landing page

Scope: the whole marketing site (one route). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: daily desktop Spotify Premium listeners on macOS (Apple Silicon) / Windows. Action: download. Proof: the live, interactive app window. Constraints: no invented claims, demo music is made up and labelled, disclaimer stays.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a quiet record sleeve in the ECM tradition: a vast muted photographic field, tiny precise label type, silence as the luxury. It refuses the category's dark-glass app page with a neon accent and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool matte-board grey ground (never cream), graphite ink, one muted tint taken live from the cover that plays. Jost set light for display, widely tracked small caps for labels. Square corners, hairline rules, no cards, no glow. The "photograph" is a live WebGL sea-and-mist horizon.

STORY: The visitor sees a calm horizon, reads "Spotify, quieter.", watches the real-feeling Lowkey window rise out of the mist, plays it, resizes it, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed mist horizon with the horizon at ~one third from the bottom; tracked LOWKEY label and a solid ink Download top right; "Spotify, quieter." light at 6rem upper left with one line and two download buttons; the top ~140px of the live app window emerging from the sea at the bottom edge. Signature interaction: the cursor moves a pale sun through the mist, and the music playing in the window re-tints the whole sky; dragging the window edge reflows tiles that glide into place only once you stop.

FORM: The Quiet Sleeve, my top-ranked grounded candidate (position 1), chosen by the user over the roll. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
